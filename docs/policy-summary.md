# Travel policy summary (sanitised)

This is a generic travel policy summary for a consultancy. It contains no names, no numbers and no client data.

## Booking and approval

- Perk trips need a cost centre and a trip description. Then press Request Approval.
- Facilities approve the trip.
- The trip description must state who approved the trip, who pays (the client or the company), and the city or area of the trip.

## Per diem

- The per diem covers meals for all travel days.
- The per diem changes when the hotel includes breakfast.

## Flights

- Book economy class for short flights.
- Long-haul flights may use economy comfort.
- Prefer refundable fares (FlexiTravel) for long-haul flights.

## Local transport

- Use the company Uber for Business account for local transport.
- Set the client name as the expense code on each ride.

## Hotels

- Confirm hotel bookings 48 hours before arrival.

## After the trip

- Register the trip in the HR system.
- File all receipts with the company VAT number.
